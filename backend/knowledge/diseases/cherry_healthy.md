# Cherry - Healthy

The cherry leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

Keep cherries healthy by pruning for airflow, removing fallen leaves and fruit, avoiding overhead watering late in the day, and watching for early powdery mildew or leaf spots in warm, humid weather.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
