# Corn (Maize) - Healthy

The maize leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

For healthy maize, use recommended hybrids, rotate crops, manage residue, give balanced fertiliser (especially nitrogen), and maintain adequate soil moisture during tasseling and grain fill.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
