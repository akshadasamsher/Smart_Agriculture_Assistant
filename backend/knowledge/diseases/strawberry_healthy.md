# Strawberry - Healthy

The strawberry leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

For healthy strawberries, use disease-free plants, space them for airflow, use mulch to keep fruit off the soil, water at the base in the morning, and remove old, dead leaves and rotting fruit.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
