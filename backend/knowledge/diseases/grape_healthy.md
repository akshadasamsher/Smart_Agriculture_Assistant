# Grape - Healthy

The grape leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

To keep grapevines healthy, prune for airflow and sun exposure, remove mummified berries and infected canes, avoid wetting the canopy, and prune in dry weather with clean tools.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
