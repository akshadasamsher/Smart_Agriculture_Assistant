# Potato - Early Blight

Early blight of potato is caused by the fungus Alternaria solani. Older leaves develop dark brown spots with concentric rings, like a target, often with a yellow border. Badly affected leaves yellow and die, and tubers can develop dark, sunken spots.

The fungus survives in crop residue and soil and spreads by wind and rain splash. It is favoured by warm weather with alternating wet and dry spells, and is worse on plants stressed by low nitrogen or drought.

Rotate crops for at least two to three years, use certified seed, remove infected debris, keep plants well fed and irrigated, and hill soil properly to protect tubers. Avoid overhead irrigation late in the day.

If disease is progressing, protective fungicide may be considered. Chemical treatment, if needed, should follow the product label and local regulations; confirm the right product, timing and dose with a local agricultural extension office or plant-protection officer.
