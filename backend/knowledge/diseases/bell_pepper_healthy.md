# Bell Pepper - Healthy

The bell pepper leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

For healthy peppers, use clean seed, rotate crops, water at the base, give steady moisture, and avoid handling plants when wet. Balanced feeding with enough calcium helps prevent fruit problems.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
