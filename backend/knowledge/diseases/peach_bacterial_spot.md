# Peach - Bacterial Spot

Bacterial spot of peach is caused by Xanthomonas arboricola pv. pruni. Leaves develop small, angular, water-soaked spots that turn purple or brown, and the centres can fall out, giving a shot-hole look. Heavy infection causes yellowing and early leaf drop. Fruit may have pitted, cracked or rough spots.

The bacteria overwinter in twig lesions and buds and spread by wind-driven rain. Warm, wet, windy weather increases outbreaks, and trees stressed by poor nutrition or drought are more susceptible.

Plant tolerant varieties, keep trees well fed and watered, prune for good airflow, and avoid excess nitrogen. Avoid overhead irrigation where possible.

Control is mainly preventive, because spread is hard to stop once it begins. Chemical treatment, if needed, should follow the product label and local regulations; confirm the right product, timing and dose with a local agricultural extension office or plant-protection officer.
