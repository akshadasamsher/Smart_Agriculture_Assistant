# Peach - Healthy

The peach leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

For healthy peach trees, prune yearly for light and airflow, maintain balanced nutrition, avoid water stress, and keep fallen leaves and fruit cleared away.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
