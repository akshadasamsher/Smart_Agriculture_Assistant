# Strawberry - Leaf Scorch

Leaf scorch of strawberry is caused by the fungus Diplocarpon earlianum. Small purple to dark red spots form on the upper leaf surface and may grow together, making leaves look scorched and brown at the edges. Severe cases weaken plants and reduce yield.

The fungus survives on old infected leaves and spreads by splashing water. Warm, humid, wet weather and dense, overcrowded beds increase the risk.

Remove old, infected leaves after harvest, avoid overcrowding, and use drip irrigation or water in the morning so leaves dry quickly. Replant with healthy, resistant plants and rotate beds.

Mild infection is usually tolerated. Heavy infection may need protection; chemical treatment, if needed, should follow the product label and local regulations; confirm the right product, timing and dose with a local agricultural extension office or plant-protection officer.
