# Potato - Healthy

The potato leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

To keep potatoes healthy, use certified disease-free seed tubers, rotate with non-solanaceous crops, hill properly, maintain even moisture, and remove volunteer potatoes and crop debris.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
