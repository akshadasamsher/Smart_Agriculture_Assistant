# Apple - Healthy

The apple leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

To keep apple trees healthy, prune for light and airflow, clear fallen leaves and fruit in autumn, water at the base rather than over the leaves, and feed according to a soil test. Choose disease-resistant varieties where possible.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
