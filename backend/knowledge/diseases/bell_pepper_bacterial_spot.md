# Bell Pepper - Bacterial Spot

Bacterial spot of pepper is caused by Xanthomonas bacteria. Leaves show small, water-soaked spots that turn brown with a yellow halo and may look raised or scabby; badly affected leaves yellow and drop. Fruit can develop rough, raised, scabby spots, which lowers quality and exposes fruit to sunscald.

The bacteria are carried on seed and plant debris and spread by splashing rain, irrigation water, wet hands and tools. Warm, humid, wet weather speeds up outbreaks.

Use clean seed and healthy transplants, rotate with non-host crops for two to three years, and avoid overhead watering. Do not work in the field when plants are wet, and clean tools. Remove and destroy badly infected plants and crop residue after harvest.

Disease is hard to cure once established, so prevention matters most. Copper-based products are sometimes used for protection. Chemical treatment, if needed, should follow the product label and local regulations; confirm the right product, timing and dose with a local agricultural extension office or plant-protection officer.
