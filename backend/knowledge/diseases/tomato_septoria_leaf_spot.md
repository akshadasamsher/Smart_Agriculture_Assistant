# Tomato - Septoria Leaf Spot

Septoria leaf spot is caused by the fungus Septoria lycopersici. Many small, round spots appear on lower leaves, with grey-white centres and dark edges, often with tiny black dots in the middle. Leaves yellow and drop, starting from the bottom, which can expose fruit to sunscald.

The fungus survives in plant debris and spreads by rain splash and handling. Warm, wet, humid weather favours it, and fruit is usually not directly affected.

Remove infected lower leaves, mulch around plants, water at the base, stake for airflow, rotate crops, and clean up debris at the end of the season.

Start control early, when first spots appear. Chemical treatment, if needed, should follow the product label and local regulations; confirm the right product, timing and dose with a local agricultural extension office or plant-protection officer.
