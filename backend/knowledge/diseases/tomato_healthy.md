# Tomato - Healthy

The tomato leaf in the photo looks healthy: an even green colour, no spots, lesions, powdery growth, curling or distortion. The model did not find signs of disease.

For healthy tomatoes, stake plants, mulch to prevent soil splash, water at the base, rotate crops, and remove old leaves and debris. Use disease-free seed and transplants.

Keep scouting weekly, especially after rain or humid spells, and check the undersides of leaves. If spots, curling or discolouration appear later, remove a sample leaf, take a clear photo and re-check, or ask a local extension officer.
