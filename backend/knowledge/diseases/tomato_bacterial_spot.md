# Tomato - Bacterial Spot

Bacterial spot of tomato is caused by Xanthomonas bacteria. Leaves show small, dark, water-soaked spots that turn brown with a yellow halo, and can look ragged as spots dry out. Fruit can develop small, raised, scabby spots, which lowers quality.

The bacteria travel on seed, transplants and plant debris, and spread by splashing rain, irrigation water and handling. Warm, humid and wet conditions favour outbreaks.

Use clean seed and healthy transplants, rotate crops, stake or trellis plants, avoid overhead watering and handling wet plants, and remove badly infected plants and residue after harvest.

Once established, it is hard to control, so prevention is key. Chemical treatment, if needed, should follow the product label and local regulations; confirm the right product, timing and dose with a local agricultural extension office or plant-protection officer.
