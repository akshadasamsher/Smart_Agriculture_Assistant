# Cotton

Cotton is a warm-season fibre crop needing a long frost-free growing period with temperatures of 21-30°C and abundant sunshine. It is relatively drought-tolerant but yields best with consistent moisture during boll development.

It grows best in deep, well-drained black or alluvial soils with a pH of 6.0-7.5, and is notably tolerant of moderately saline soils compared to many crops. Rainfall of 60-120cm during the growing season, or irrigation, supports good yields.

Sown at the start of the warm season, with row spacing of 60-90cm and plant spacing of 15-30cm depending on variety. It is a heavy feeder of nitrogen and potassium, particularly during the boll-formation stage.

Matures in 150-180 days. Typical seed cotton yields range from 1.5-2.5 tonnes/hectare (roughly 500-800 kg/ha of lint) under good management.
