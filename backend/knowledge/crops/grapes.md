# Grapes

Grapevines prefer a climate with warm, dry summers and moderate winters, with an optimal growing-season temperature of 15-30°C. They need a period of winter chilling for dormancy in temperate varieties, and abundant sunshine for good fruit sugar development.

Well-drained soils are essential -- sandy loam to gravelly soils with a pH of 6.0-7.0 work well, since grapevines are highly sensitive to waterlogging. Moderate rainfall (65-100cm) or controlled irrigation is preferred over heavy, uncontrolled rain, which promotes fungal disease and dilutes fruit sugars.

Propagated from cuttings or grafted rootstock, planted during dormancy, and trained onto a trellis system with spacing of roughly 2-3m between vines. Vines take 3 years to reach commercial bearing and can remain productive for decades.

Potassium is particularly important for fruit quality. Yields vary greatly by system and variety, typically 15-30 tonnes/hectare for table grapes under good management.
