# Maize (Corn)

Maize is a warm-season cereal, growing best at 21-27°C with a long, frost-free growing season (roughly 100-140 frost-free days depending on variety). It is sensitive to frost at both planting and maturity.

It grows well on a range of well-drained, fertile soils, ideally deep loams with a pH of 5.5-7.0. It needs moderate to high rainfall (50-100cm during the growing season) or irrigation, with water stress during silking/tasseling causing the biggest yield losses.

Sown at the start of the warm season, in rows spaced 60-75cm apart with plant spacing of 20-25cm. Maize is a heavy nitrogen feeder, typically requiring split nitrogen application (basal + knee-high + tasseling stages) for best yields.

Matures in 90-120 days depending on variety. Typical yields range from 4-10 tonnes/hectare under rainfed conditions, higher (10+ tonnes/hectare) with irrigation and hybrid seed.
