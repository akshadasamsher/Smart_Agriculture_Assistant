# Lentil (Masoor)

Lentil is a cool-season pulse, growing best at 18-30°C during vegetative growth but tolerating cooler temperatures (down to near 0°C for short periods) better than most pulses. It is fairly drought-tolerant once established.

It performs well on a range of well-drained soils, from sandy loam to clay loam, with a pH of 6.0-7.5, but is sensitive to waterlogging and salinity. It relies mainly on residual moisture and low rainfall (40-60cm annual) rather than heavy irrigation.

Typically sown in the post-monsoon/winter (rabi) season, at a seed rate of 30-40 kg/ha with row spacing of 25-30cm. As a legume, it needs only a small starter dose of nitrogen alongside phosphorus.

Matures in 100-130 days. Typical yields range from 0.8-1.2 tonnes/hectare, with light irrigation at flowering/pod-filling improving results where feasible.
