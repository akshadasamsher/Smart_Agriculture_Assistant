# Orange

Orange is a subtropical citrus fruit tree, growing best at 15-30°C with mild winters; it tolerates light frost better than some citrus but sustained freezing damages fruit and young growth.

It grows best in well-drained sandy loam soils with a pH of 5.5-7.5, and is sensitive to waterlogging and salinity. Annual rainfall of 100-150cm, or equivalent irrigation, is needed, with consistent moisture important during fruit development.

Propagated by grafting onto disease-resistant rootstock, spaced 4-6m apart. Trees begin bearing 3-4 years after planting and remain productive for 20-30+ years with good care.

Requires balanced NPK, with nitrogen particularly important for vegetative growth and fruit yield. Mature orchards typically yield 15-30 tonnes/hectare under good management and irrigation.
