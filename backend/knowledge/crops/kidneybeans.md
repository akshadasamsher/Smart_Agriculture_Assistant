# Kidney Beans (Rajma)

Kidney beans are a cool-to-warm season legume, growing best at 15-25°C; they are sensitive to both frost and extreme heat above 30°C, which reduces pod set.

They prefer well-drained loamy soils with a pH of 6.0-7.5 and good organic matter content. Moderate, even rainfall (60-90cm during the growing season) is ideal -- they are sensitive to both drought stress during flowering and waterlogging.

Sown either at the start of the monsoon or, in cooler/higher-altitude areas, as a spring crop, with row spacing of 40-50cm and plant spacing of 10cm. Being a legume, it needs only modest nitrogen (mainly as a starter, since it fixes its own) plus phosphorus and potassium.

Matures in 90-120 days. Typical yields range from 1.5-2.5 tonnes/hectare under good management with adequate moisture at flowering and pod-filling.
