# Rice

Rice thrives in warm, humid climates with standing water for much of the
growing season. It prefers temperatures between 20-35°C and does best with
high rainfall (over 150mm) or reliable irrigation, since paddies need to
stay flooded during most of the vegetative stage.

Ideal soil is clay or clay-loam that holds water well, with a pH between
5.5 and 7.0. Nitrogen is the most yield-limiting nutrient; split nitrogen
application (basal + tillering + panicle initiation) gives better results
than a single dose.

Typical sowing window is with the onset of the monsoon (June-July in most
of South Asia) for the main season crop, or during the dry season with
irrigation. Transplant seedlings 20-25 days after nursery sowing, spaced
about 20x15cm apart. Expect maturity in 110-150 days depending on variety.

Typical yields range from 3-6 tonnes/hectare under good management, higher
for high-yielding or hybrid varieties with proper fertilization.
