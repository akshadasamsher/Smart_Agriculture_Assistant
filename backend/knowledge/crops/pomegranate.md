# Pomegranate

Pomegranate is well suited to semi-arid and Mediterranean-type climates, tolerating both summer heat and mild winter cold, with an optimal growing temperature of 25-35°C. It needs a hot, dry summer for good fruit quality and coloring.

It is notably tolerant of a wide range of soils, including poor, saline, and calcareous soils, preferring a pH of 5.5-7.5, and is more drought-tolerant than most fruit trees once established. Annual rainfall of 50-75cm, or supplemental irrigation, is sufficient, with irrigation particularly important during fruit development.

Propagated from cuttings, spaced roughly 4-5m apart. Trees begin bearing 2-3 years after planting and remain productive for 15-20+ years.

Requires moderate NPK, with potassium important for fruit quality. Mature orchards yield roughly 10-15 tonnes/hectare under good management.
