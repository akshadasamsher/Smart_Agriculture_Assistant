# Banana

Banana is a tropical crop needing warm, humid, frost-free conditions year-round, with optimal temperatures of 26-30°C. It is sensitive to cold and stops growing below 15°C.

It grows best in deep, well-drained, fertile loamy soil rich in organic matter, with a pH of 6.0-7.5. Banana is a heavy water user, needing 1200-2500mm of well-distributed rainfall annually or regular irrigation; it does not tolerate waterlogging or drought well.

Propagated from suckers or tissue-culture plantlets, spaced roughly 2x2m to 3x3m depending on variety and density goals. Planting is usually timed with the onset of rains. The crop is a heavy feeder, especially of potassium and nitrogen, and benefits from regular mulching to conserve moisture.

Time to first harvest is typically 10-15 months after planting. Yields of 30-60 tonnes/hectare are achievable under good management with irrigation and fertilization.
