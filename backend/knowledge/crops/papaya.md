# Papaya

Papaya is a fast-growing tropical fruit crop, requiring warm, frost-free conditions year-round with an optimal temperature range of 21-33°C. It is highly sensitive to cold and waterlogging, with even brief frost or standing water often killing plants.

It grows best in well-drained, fertile sandy loam soils with a pH of 6.0-6.5 and good organic matter. It needs regular moisture (100-150cm annual rainfall or irrigation) but must have excellent drainage, as its roots are very prone to rot.

Propagated from seed or tissue culture, spaced roughly 1.8-2.5m apart. It is one of the fastest fruiting trees, bearing within 8-10 months of planting, though commercial productive life is typically only 3-4 years.

A heavy feeder, especially of nitrogen and potassium, benefiting from regular light fertilization. Yields are high for a fruit crop, typically 40-60 tonnes/hectare under good management.
