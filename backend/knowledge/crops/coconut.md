# Coconut

Coconut is a tropical palm requiring consistently warm, humid conditions year-round (temperatures of 27-32°C) and does not tolerate frost or prolonged cold. It thrives in coastal and near-coastal areas, tolerating salt spray and sandy soils.

It grows best in well-drained sandy loam or coastal alluvial soils with a pH of 5.2-8.0, and needs abundant, well-distributed rainfall (150-250cm annually) or irrigation, as it does not tolerate prolonged drought during establishment.

Propagated from seed nuts/seedlings, spaced widely at 7.5-9m apart given its large mature canopy. It is a long-term perennial crop, starting to bear nuts around 5-6 years after planting and remaining productive for 60+ years.

Requires regular potassium and nitrogen fertilization, especially on sandy soils. A mature, well-managed palm can yield 80-120 nuts/year.
