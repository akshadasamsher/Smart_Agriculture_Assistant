# Pigeon Pea (Arhar/Tur)

Pigeon pea is a warm-season, deep-rooted pulse suited to semi-arid regions, growing best at 20-30°C. Its deep root system makes it notably drought-tolerant once established.

It tolerates a wide range of soils, from sandy loam to clay, with a preferred pH of 5.0-7.0, and tolerates poorer soils better than many pulses, though it does not tolerate waterlogging. It performs well with modest rainfall of 60-100cm during the growing season.

Sown at the start of the monsoon, in rows spaced 60-75cm apart (wider for long-duration varieties), with a seed rate of 15-20 kg/ha. As a legume, it fixes its own nitrogen and mainly needs phosphorus as a starter.

A relatively long-duration crop, maturing in 150-270 days depending on variety (short vs. long duration types). Typical yields range from 0.8-1.5 tonnes/hectare.
