# Mango

Mango is a tropical/subtropical fruit tree, growing best with a distinct dry season for flower induction and temperatures of 24-30°C during the growing season. It tolerates heat well but is sensitive to frost, especially when young.

It grows on a wide range of soils but prefers deep, well-drained sandy loam with a pH of 5.5-7.5, and is sensitive to waterlogging. Annual rainfall of 75-250cm is suitable provided there's a distinct dry period of 2-3 months before flowering; irrigation should be reduced or withheld during this period.

Propagated by grafting, planted at the start of the rainy season, spaced 8-10m apart for standard varieties (closer for high-density planting). Trees begin bearing 4-5 years after planting (earlier for grafted stock) and remain productive for decades.

Potassium is particularly important for fruit quality and size. Mature orchards yield roughly 8-15 tonnes/hectare under good management, though mango is prone to biennial (alternate-year) bearing.
