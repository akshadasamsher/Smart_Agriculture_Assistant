# Muskmelon

Muskmelon is a warm-season vine crop needing high temperatures (24-32°C) and plenty of sunshine for good sugar development; it is sensitive to frost and cool, cloudy weather.

It prefers well-drained sandy loam soils with a pH of 6.0-7.0 and good organic matter. It needs moderate, consistent moisture (particularly during fruit development) but is sensitive to waterlogging and excess moisture near harvest, which reduces fruit sweetness and shelf life.

Sown after the last frost when soil has warmed, in rows or hills spaced 1-2m apart depending on variety and trellising. It is a heavy feeder of potassium, which is important for fruit sweetness and quality, alongside moderate nitrogen.

Matures in 80-100 days from sowing. Typical yields range from 15-25 tonnes/hectare under good management with adequate irrigation and pollination.
