# Chickpea (Gram)

Chickpea is a cool-season pulse, growing best at 10-25°C, and is notably drought-tolerant once established, making it suitable for semi-arid regions. It is sensitive to waterlogging and high humidity, which promote fungal disease.

It prefers well-drained sandy loam to clay loam soils with a pH of 6.0-7.5, and does not tolerate saline or waterlogged soils well. It generally relies on residual soil moisture and low rainfall (60-90cm annual, mostly outside the growing season) rather than heavy irrigation.

Typically sown in the post-monsoon/winter (rabi) season, at a seed rate of 60-100 kg/ha and row spacing of 30-45cm. Like other legumes, it fixes its own nitrogen and mainly needs phosphorus and potassium as starter fertilizer.

Matures in 90-120 days. Typical yields range from 0.8-1.5 tonnes/hectare, higher with irrigation at critical growth stages (flowering, pod filling).
