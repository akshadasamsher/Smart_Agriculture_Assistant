# Mung Bean (Green Gram)

Mung bean is a warm-season, short-duration pulse, growing best at 25-35°C, and is reasonably drought-tolerant once established, though sensitive to waterlogging.

It grows well on well-drained sandy loam to loam soils with a pH of 6.2-7.2. Moderate rainfall of 60-75cm during its short growing season, or light irrigation, is sufficient.

Can be sown in kharif (monsoon), or as a short summer catch crop between main seasons, at a seed rate of 15-20 kg/ha with row spacing of 30cm. As a legume, it fixes its own nitrogen and needs only a small starter dose plus phosphorus.

One of the fastest-maturing pulses, ready in 60-90 days. Typical yields range from 0.5-0.8 tonnes/hectare, with its short duration making it useful for fitting into multiple cropping systems.
