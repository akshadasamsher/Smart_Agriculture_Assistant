# Coffee

Coffee (typically Arabica or Robusta) is grown in tropical highland climates. Arabica prefers cooler temperatures of 15-24°C and higher altitudes (600-2000m), while Robusta tolerates warmer lowland conditions (24-30°C). Both need protection from frost and strong direct sun, often grown under shade trees.

Well-drained, deep, fertile volcanic or loamy soils with a pH of 6.0-6.5 are ideal. Coffee needs consistent rainfall of 150-250cm annually, well distributed with a short dry spell to trigger flowering.

Propagated from seedlings, planted at the start of the rainy season, spaced roughly 2x2m to 2.5x2.5m. It takes 3-4 years to reach first bearing and remains productive for 20-30 years with proper pruning and shade management.

Requires balanced fertilization, particularly nitrogen and potassium. Yields vary widely by system but average roughly 1-2.5 tonnes/hectare of processed beans under good management.
